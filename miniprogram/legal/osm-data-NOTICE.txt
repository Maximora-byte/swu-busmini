Public source: https://github.com/Maximora-byte/swu-busmini

OSM mapping is a community map reference, not a field survey or verified
building entrance. Geometry bounds centers may not be entrances. Campus-guide
aliases and service descriptions were supplied by the user from Southwest
University campus forum materials; original photographs, QR codes, contact
information and private evidence are not redistributed here.

The OSM-derived address database remains separate from operator-published bus
routes and transient Tencent native-map selections. Tencent POI data is not
bulk downloaded or copied into this address database. No OSM tile service or
public geocoding service is contacted by autocomplete at runtime.
